A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Property management questions

What areas does Summit Peak serve?+

We primarily serve Colorado Springs and El Paso County, including Fountain, Manitou Springs, Monument, Palmer Lake, Calhan, Falcon, Peyton, and Black Forest. Contact us to discuss your property’s location.

What property management services do I need?+

The right support depends on your property and how involved you want to be. Tell us whether you need leasing help, ongoing management, or owner guidance. We’ll discuss the services before you decide.

How do I get started with Summit Peak?+

Call 719-623-7432 or email contact@summitpeakpm.com. Share your property’s location and the support you need so we can discuss your next steps.

How can I ask about available rental homes?+

Contact us by phone or email to ask about current availability. Include your preferred location, budget, and move-in date. You can also choose “Rental availability” in the inquiry form.

How do residents get help with their home?+

For routine questions, call or email us, or choose “Resident question” in the inquiry form. For urgent maintenance, follow the contact instructions in your lease or resident portal. Do not use this general inquiry form for emergencies.
