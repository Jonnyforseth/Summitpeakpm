LEAVING THE AREA

## Keep your home. Simplify the handoff.

A new duty station does not have to mean handling renter calls from another time zone. Summit Peak manages communication directly and helps you prepare for rental ownership while you are away.

- Discuss property readiness before departure.

- Agree on updates and owner approvals.
