MILITARY & VETERAN RENTERS

A rental search is easier when you begin with the details that shape your everyday life. For military members and veterans moving to Colorado Springs, those details often include a duty location, a changing schedule, and a move that has several pieces in motion.

Summit Peak Property Management Colorado Springs & El Paso County

## Start with the destination you actually need to reach

Colorado Springs is connected to several military communities. Fort Carson, Peterson Space Force Base, and Schriever Space Force Base are distinct destinations. Confirm the location you will report to before choosing where to focus your search. The [Fort Carson housing office](https://home.army.mil/carson/allservices/housing)  and [Peterson and Schriever resource directory](https://www.petersonschriever.spaceforce.mil/About-Us/Resources/)  are useful official starting points.

Compare an individual property with your actual route and schedule. A neighborhood name or a straight-line distance does not establish a commute. Check access instructions, likely travel times, and the practical needs of other household members.

## Keep your search flexible across El Paso County

Summit Peak serves Colorado Springs and surrounding El Paso County communities, including Fountain, Manitou Springs, Monument, Palmer Lake, Falcon, Peyton, Black Forest, and Calhan. Tell us where you need to go rather than assuming one community will be the best fit.

Make two lists: requirements and preferences. Your move-in window, monthly budget, needed space, and any pet considerations belong in the conversation early. Outdoor space or a particular layout may be preferences you can compare once actual homes are available. Availability changes; this guide does not advertise any specific property.

## Ask about the process before sending documents

Request the current application criteria, fees, required documents, and review steps for the property you are considering. Knowing the process first can prevent unnecessary applications and repeated requests for information.

Use the verified submission method supplied by the manager for identity and financial documents. The general contact form is for an introduction, not a full rental application. Share your preferred dates and questions there, then follow the instructions provided for the next stage.

Ask how an application decision is communicated, what happens after approval, and which items must be completed before keys are released. Clear expectations help both the renter and the manager prepare.

## Plan for a change in orders

If orders change, contact your property manager promptly. Eligible servicemembers may have residential lease-termination protections under the SCRA when qualifying circumstances apply. Proper notice and supporting documents matter. Consult the [Department of Justice’s military housing-rights guidance](https://www.justice.gov/servicemembers/financial-and-housing-rights-0)  and your installation legal assistance office for your situation.

Do not treat a casual conversation about moving as confirmation that a lease has ended. Ask how to submit documents and how the effective date will be confirmed. Keep copies of your communications.

## Know who to contact after move-in

A smooth rental experience continues after you receive the keys. Confirm where routine questions go, how to report maintenance, and which instructions to follow for an urgent problem. Keep that information somewhere everyone in the household can find.

At Summit Peak, communication runs through the management team rather than separate conversations with the owner. Our veteran-owned background is part of our commitment to personal service. Military members, veterans, and civilians are all welcome to [ask about their rental needs](https://www.summitpeakpm.com/#contact) .

This guide provides general information. For questions about a lease, military rights, or legal action, speak with a qualified legal professional. Confirm service and rent-protection terms directly with Summit Peak.
